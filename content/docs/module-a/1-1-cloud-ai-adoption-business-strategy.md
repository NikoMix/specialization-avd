## What the Auditor Checks

The auditor verifies that the AVD engagement is grounded in a **documented
customer business strategy** — not a tactical IT request. The strategy must
tie the AVD investment to specific, measurable business outcomes (cost,
security, workforce flexibility, M&amp;A integration, regulatory).

**Typical questions:**
- Is there a written cloud & AI adoption strategy for this customer?
- How does AVD support the strategy?
- Was the strategy reviewed and signed by a customer business sponsor (not just IT)?

## Evidence Guidance

### Document structure

A 4–8 page document that includes:

1. **Business motivation** — why the customer is investing in cloud / AVD now
2. **Business outcomes** — measurable goals (e.g. *retire RDS by Q4, reduce
   endpoint refresh cost by 30%, enable hybrid workforce of N users*)
3. **Stakeholders** — customer-side business sponsor, IT sponsor, EUC owner
4. **AVD linkage** — how AVD specifically delivers the outcomes vs.
   alternatives (Windows 365, on-prem VDI refresh, do-nothing)
5. **Success criteria** — what "done" looks like
6. **Signatures** — customer business sponsor, your engagement lead

### Common evidence shape

- A short executive document signed by the customer's CIO / Head of Workplace
  in addition to your engagement lead.
- Microsoft CAF Strategy and Plan workbook outputs co-authored with the
  customer.

## Common Gaps

| Gap | Remediation |
|---|---|
| Strategy is IT-authored, no business sponsor signature | Add a 1-page exec summary and obtain CIO / Head of Workplace sign-off |
| Strategy doesn't mention AVD by name | Add an AVD section linking specific outcomes to AVD capabilities |
| Outcomes are vague ("modernise EUC") | Quantify: target user count, target cost, target retirement date |
| Document exists as a slide deck only | Convert to a signed PDF document; decks alone are weak evidence |
