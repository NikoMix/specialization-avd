## What is the AVD Advanced Specialization?

The **Azure Virtual Desktop Advanced Specialization** is a Microsoft Partner
credential that validates deep technical and delivery capability in designing,
deploying, and operating Azure Virtual Desktop (AVD) for customers. It signals
to customers and to Microsoft that your organisation:

- Has **proven delivery** of AVD workloads to real customers (Native AVD,
  Citrix on Azure, or Horizon on Azure)
- Holds the **Solutions Partner for Infrastructure (Azure)** designation
- Follows a **repeatable, well-architected** delivery methodology
- Has passed a rigorous **independent third-party audit** (ISSI) validating
  both your engagement processes and your customer outcomes

{{% alert type="tip" %}}
Partners who hold the AVD Advanced Specialization typically appear higher
in Microsoft Partner Finder, qualify for additional co-sell benefits, and
gain access to Microsoft-funded marketing programmes for EUC / modern
workplace workloads.
{{% /alert %}}

## Why pursue this specialization?

| Benefit | Details |
|---|---|
| **Differentiation** | Stand out among EUC / modern workplace partners with an audited credential |
| **Co-sell eligibility** | Priority co-sell referrals for AVD opportunities |
| **Marketing assets** | Use the specialization badge and Microsoft-approved collateral |
| **Partner Finder visibility** | Appear in customer-facing searches filtered by AVD |
| **Customer confidence** | Demonstrate credibility backed by an independent audit |

## Specialization scope (V2.7.1)

This guide covers the audit checklist **V2.7.1** (valid Jan 1 → Jun 30, 2026).
**V2.8 PREVIEW** lands Jun 1, 2026; see the
[Audit Process](/docs/audit-process) page for transition guidance.

| Module | Scope | Validity |
|---|---|---|
| **Module A** | Azure Essentials Cloud Foundation — shared across all Infrastructure-Azure specializations | **2 years** |
| **Module B** | Azure Virtual Desktop workload — solution-specific delivery and operations | **1 year** |

## How this guide helps

This repository is structured as an **engagement toolkit**. Each page maps to
either an audit control or an engagement phase, and contains:

1. **What the auditor checks** *(control pages)* — exact criteria from V2.7.1
2. **Required evidence** — structured checklist of accepted document types
3. **Evidence status table** — assign owners, set deadlines, track status
4. **Common gaps** — practical remediation for known issues
5. **Engagement guidance** *(playbook pages)* — when to use, inputs needed,
   step-by-step, customer-ready output

Use this guide alongside your Microsoft Partner Center portal and your
internal project management tooling.
