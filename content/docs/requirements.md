Every requirement below must be satisfied **before** requesting the audit.
Confirm with your Microsoft Partner Development Manager (PDM).
{{% /alert %}}

## Requirement 1 – Active Solutions Partner designation

Your organisation must hold an **active Solutions Partner for Infrastructure
(Azure)** designation.

| Designation | How to verify |
|---|---|
| Solutions Partner for **Infrastructure (Azure)** | Partner Center → Overview → Membership |

> ✅ **Evidence needed:** Screenshot of active designation from Partner Center,
> or the Partner Center PDF report showing designation status.

---

## Requirement 2 – Customer evidence

Your organisation must have delivered **2 unique customer engagements** in the
**last 12 months** (the "Timeframe"). The rules:

| # | Rule |
|---|---|
| 1 | **At least 1 customer must be Native Azure Virtual Desktop** |
| 2 | The 2nd customer may be Native AVD, **Citrix on Azure**, or **Horizon on Azure** |
| 3 | Each engagement must have reached **Go-Live** (production serving end users) within the Timeframe |
| 4 | Customer sign-off must be captured |

{{% alert type="tip" %}}
Customer Case Studies and Customer Sign-Off documents are the two artefacts
the auditor will request per customer. Anonymisation is acceptable for
internal copies; the auditor may verify customer association via Partner
Center data.
{{% /alert %}}

> ✅ **Evidence needed (per customer):** Customer Case Study + Customer
> Sign-Off + (if available) project artefacts demonstrating Go-Live.

---

## Requirement 3 – Recommended certifications

Not strictly gated at pre-qualification, but auditors expect to see relevant
team certifications surfaced as part of Module A.3.2 (Plan for Skilling) and
Module B technical depth. The standard set:

| Certification | Exam code | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|
| Azure Virtual Desktop Specialty | **AZ-140** | Core AVD design / operate / monitor |
| Azure Administrator Associate | **AZ-104** | Foundation for AVD landing zone |
| Microsoft 365 family | MS-100 / MS-700 family | M365 Apps + Teams interop with AVD |

> ✅ **Evidence needed:** Microsoft Learn transcripts or certification
> verification links. Prepare a mapping table (Name → Certification → Expiry).

---

## Requirement 4 – Third-Party Remote Audit (ISSI)

Your organisation must pass a **third-party remote audit** conducted by
**ISSI (Information Security Systems International, LLC)** — the
Microsoft-approved auditor for this specialization.

1. Confirm pre-qualification (Requirements 1–3 above are met)
2. Request audit via Partner Center → Advanced Specializations
3. Receive ISSI auditor assignment and kickoff call scheduling
4. Choose audit scope: **Module B only** (4h, $2,400) **or** **Module A + B** (8h, $3,600)
5. Submit evidence package to the auditor
6. Complete the audit interview / review session
7. Address any remediation findings (if required)
8. Receive audit pass confirmation

{{% alert type="tip" %}}
Module A is **shared** across all Infrastructure-Azure Solutions Partner
specializations and is valid **2 years** once passed. If your organisation
already passed Module A within the last 2 years for another specialization
(e.g. AKS, SAP on Azure, Hybrid Cloud), choose **Module B only** to save
time and $1,200.
{{% /alert %}}

> ✅ **Evidence needed:** This entire repository constitutes your evidence
> preparation package. Use [Module A](/docs/module-a/1-1-cloud-ai-adoption-business-strategy)
> and [Module B](/docs/module-b/1-1-workload-assessment) pages for control-level
> evidence.
