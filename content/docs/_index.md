Work through the guide in order the first time: **Overview** explains what the
specialization is and why it is worth pursuing, **Pre-Qualification
Requirements** confirms your organisation is eligible, and **Audit Process**
walks the ISSI-led remote audit end to end.

After that, the two control sections are the working surface. **Module A —
Azure Essentials** covers the seven shared controls that any Solutions Partner
for Infrastructure (Azure) specialization reuses. **Module B — Azure Virtual
Desktop** covers the seven AVD-specific controls and is re-audited every year.

The **Engagement Playbook** is the delivery side of the same material: the
offering, the discovery kit, the reference architectures, and the customer
deliverable templates that produce audit evidence as a by-product of doing the
work properly.
