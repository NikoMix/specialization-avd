## Overview

The Advanced Specialization audit is conducted by **ISSI (Information Security
Systems International, LLC)** — the Microsoft-approved auditor for this
specialization. The audit is performed remotely (video call + document review).

| Scope | Duration | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Module B only** | 4 hours | **$2,400** |
| **Module A + Module B** | 8 hours | **$3,600** |

| Pass | Validity |
|---|---|
| Module A | **2 calendar years** |
| Module B | **1 calendar year** |

<Aside type="tip">
  Module A is shared across all Solutions Partner Infrastructure (Azure)
  specializations. If you have passed Module A within the last 2 years for
  another specialization (AKS, SAP, Hybrid Cloud, Networking, AVS, etc.),
  choose **Module B only** to save 4 hours and $1,200.
</Aside>

<Aside type="caution">
  **Version transition window:** V2.7.1 is the active checklist through
  **Jun 30, 2026**. **V2.8 PREVIEW** lands **Jun 1, 2026**. If your audit
  is booked between Jun 1 and Jun 30, 2026, confirm with ISSI which version
  applies before submitting evidence.
</Aside>

## Audit Phases

<Steps>

1. ### Pre-Qualification Confirmation

   Before requesting the audit, confirm you meet the
   [pre-qualification requirements](../requirements/):

   - Active Solutions Partner for Infrastructure (Azure)
   - 2 qualifying customers in the last 12 months (≥1 Native AVD)
   - Recommended certifications mapped to your team

2. ### Audit Request via Partner Center

   Navigate to **Partner Center → Benefits → Advanced Specializations** and
   request the audit for _Azure Virtual Desktop_.

   You will be asked to select your audit scope (Module B only vs Module A + B).

3. ### Kickoff Call with ISSI

   Expect a 30–60 minute kickoff covering:

   - Audit process, timeline, and evidence submission format
   - Confirmation of audit scope and the 2 customer engagements you will use
     as evidence
   - Deadline for evidence submission

   **Bring:** project lead, technical / EUC lead, and (for Module A) the
   compliance / operations lead.

4. ### Evidence Collection & Submission

   Use the [Evidence Tracker](../evidence-tracker/) to manage collection
   across all controls.

   <Tabs>
     <TabItem label="Module A – Azure Essentials">
       | Control | Key Evidence |
       |---|---|
       | [A.1.1 Cloud & AI Adoption Business Strategy](../module-a/1-1-cloud-ai-adoption-business-strategy/) | Customer-signed strategy linking AVD to business outcomes |
       | [A.1.2 Cloud & AI Adoption Plan](../module-a/1-2-cloud-ai-adoption-plan/) | Wave plan / roadmap with AVD sequenced |
       | [A.2.1 Security & Governance Tooling](../module-a/2-1-security-governance-tooling/) | Defender for Cloud, Policy, Entra CA |

5. ### Auditor Review

   ISSI reviews your evidence against the V2.7.1 checklist and may request
   clarifications. Typical review duration: **5–10 business days**.

   - Respond to queries promptly (target &lt; 2 business days)
   - Assign a single point of contact
   - Keep evidence version-controlled (dated filenames)

6. ### Audit Interview

   The audit itself is **4 hours** (Module B only) or **8 hours** (Module A + B),
   conducted as a video call. The auditor walks through:

   - Each control with you and your team
   - The 2 customer engagements end-to-end
   - Sample evidence artefacts (HLD, runbook, sign-off, etc.)

7. ### Remediation (if applicable)

   If gaps are identified, ISSI provides a **remediation report**.

   <Aside type="caution">
     You typically have **30 days** to address remediation findings. Treat
     this as a priority — delays can require restarting the audit cycle.
   </Aside>

8. ### Audit Pass & Specialization Award

   Once ISSI confirms a pass, Microsoft Partner Center is updated within
   **10–15 business days**.

   **Next steps after passing:**
   - Download the specialization badge from Partner Center
   - Update marketing materials and website
   - Notify your PDM to unlock co-sell and marketing benefits
   - Diary the renewal:
     - Module A — re-audit within **2 years**
     - Module B — re-audit within **1 calendar year**

</Steps>

## Timeline Summary

| Phase | Typical Duration |
|---|---|
| Pre-qualification confirmation | 1–2 weeks |
| Audit request to ISSI assignment | 5–10 business days |
| Evidence collection (prepared) | 1–2 weeks |
| Evidence collection (unprepared) | 4–8 weeks |
| Audit interview itself | 4h (B only) / 8h (A + B) |
| ISSI review | 5–10 business days |
| Remediation (if needed) | Up to 30 days |
| Specialization badge publication | 10–15 business days |
| **Total (well-prepared partner)** | **~6–8 weeks** |
