## Pre-Qualification

### Do we need any specific certifications to pre-qualify?

Pre-qualification itself only requires the **Solutions Partner for
Infrastructure (Azure)** designation. Certifications (AZ-140 in particular)
are not gated at pre-qual but are evaluated within Module A.3.2 (Plan for
Skilling) and the Module B technical depth interview. Have at least one AZ-140
holder identified before the audit.

### What counts as "Native AVD" for the customer evidence rule?

A Native AVD engagement is one where the customer's primary virtual desktop
platform is **Azure Virtual Desktop** (host pools, session hosts, AVD service
metadata) — **not** Citrix DaaS or VMware Horizon brokered on top of Azure VMs.
The second customer may use Citrix on Azure or Horizon on Azure.

### Both our delivered engagements are Citrix on Azure — do we qualify?

No. The customer evidence rule requires **at least one Native AVD**
engagement. Plan a Native AVD engagement before requesting the audit.

### How recent must the customer engagements be?

Both engagements must have reached **Go-Live** within the **last 12 months**
(the "Timeframe") at the point of audit submission.

## Audit Process

### Who is the auditor?

**ISSI** (Information Security Systems International, LLC) — Microsoft's
approved auditor for this specialization.

### How long does the audit take?

The audit interview itself is **4 hours** (Module B only) or **8 hours**
(Module A + B). End-to-end including prep, review, and remediation, plan
**6–8 weeks** for a well-prepared partner.

### What does it cost?

**$2,400** for Module B only, **$3,600** for Module A + B combined.

### Module A was passed last year — do we have to do it again?

No. Module A is shared across all Solutions Partner for Infrastructure (Azure)
specializations and is valid **2 calendar years**. Choose **Module B only**
when you book.

### What about V2.8 PREVIEW?

V2.8 lands as a PREVIEW on **Jun 1, 2026**. V2.7.1 remains the active checklist
through **Jun 30, 2026**. Audits booked in that overlap window — confirm with
ISSI which version applies before submitting evidence.

### What format does the auditor want evidence in?

PDF, Word, Excel, and PowerPoint. Screenshots exported as PDF where possible.
Name documents with control references (e.g., `B.2.1_AVD_HLD_v2.pdf`).

### What happens if we fail the audit?

You will receive a **remediation report** itemising specific control gaps. You
typically have **30 days** to address findings. Failure to remediate in time
may require restarting the audit cycle.

## Architecture & Delivery

### Pooled multi-session or personal host pools?

Default to **pooled multi-session** (Win 11 Enterprise multi-session) for
knowledge workers — best cost and density. Use **personal** for developers,
power users, GPU workloads, or app-isolation requirements. See
[Reference Architectures](../engagement/reference-architectures/).

### Azure Files or Azure NetApp Files for FSLogix?

**Azure Files Premium** is the default for &lt; 1,000 users. Use **Azure
NetApp Files** when sub-ms latency, very high IOPS, or large profile counts
are required, or when the customer already standardises on ANF.

### Entra-joined or Hybrid joined?

**Entra-joined** wherever the app portfolio allows. **Hybrid join** when LOB
apps still need on-prem AD (Kerberos / NTLM bound).

### MSIX AppAttach vs Intune for app delivery?

**AppAttach (MSIX)** for shared apps consumed by many users — once-attached,
served to all session hosts. **Intune** for per-user apps. **Microsoft 365
Apps** uses shared-computer activation.

### How big should FSLogix profile containers be?

Plan **30 GB** per knowledge worker as a starting budget; raise for power
users or where OneDrive Known Folder Move stores significant content inside
the container. Monitor container growth and document a cleanup pattern in
your runbook.

## Engagement Delivery

### How long is a typical AVD engagement?

**6–10 weeks** for a greenfield Native AVD deployment: 1–2w discovery,
1–2w design, 1–2w PoC / pilot, 2–3w production rollout, 1–2w hypercare.

### Do we need IaC?

Yes — **A.3.1 Repeatable Deployment** requires it. Use the published
**Azure Virtual Desktop landing zone** Bicep modules as a starting point.

### What WAF pillars do auditors focus on for AVD?

For Module B.2.2, expect emphasis on **Cost** (autoscale, RIs, density),
**Performance** (RDP Shortpath, profile storage tier, region selection), and
**Reliability** (host pool redundancy, profile availability, BCDR pattern).

### Can we anonymise customer names in evidence?

Yes for internal copies. ISSI may verify customer association via Partner
Center; coordinate with your PDM on acceptable anonymisation.

## Post-Specialization

### How long is the specialization valid?

- **Module A** pass: **2 calendar years**
- **Module B** pass: **1 calendar year**

Plan a Module B re-audit each year.

### Where does the badge appear?

On your **Microsoft AppSource profile**, **Microsoft Partner Finder**, and
downloadable from Partner Center for use on your website and marketing
materials.

### What benefits unlock?

- **Co-sell priority** for AVD opportunities
- **Marketing through Microsoft (MtM)** eligibility for EUC workloads
- Access to Microsoft-led GTM programmes for modern workplace

<Aside>
  Benefits change periodically. Confirm current benefits with your PDM or on
  the [Microsoft Partner Network benefits page](https://partner.microsoft.com/en-us/partnership/partner-network/benefits).
</Aside>
